AI Voice Agent for BGV Insufficiency Resolution

A voice-driven solution that automates candidate follow-up for background verification insufficiencies, reduces analyst effort, and accelerates case resolution through structured conversation and record updates.

Version: 1.0 Prepared for: Akash / Samatha Prepared by: Shravya, Inayat, Saketh Stack: Dograh AI + Twilio + FastMCP + SQLite + Python

View related PDF

1. Design Planning Summary

Background

BGV teams often face incomplete records, invalid references, missing documents, and unresolved verification issues. Analysts spend significant time manually contacting candidates, tracking responses, and updating records.

Proposed Solution

The solution introduces an AI voice agent that automatically calls candidates when an insufficiency is detected, collects required information, updates records, and routes exceptions when needed.

2. Business Value

  • Reduces analyst effort spent on repetitive candidate follow-ups.
  • Improves insufficiency resolution turnaround time.
  • Prevents onboarding delays and supports SLA compliance.
  • Creates a standard, auditable process for every candidate interaction.

3. Functional Overview

  1. Candidate case is reviewed and an insufficiency is identified.
  2. The voice agent is triggered and a call is initiated through Twilio.
  3. The agent collects missing details through a guided conversation.
  4. Relevant records are updated and the interaction is logged.
  5. The case is resolved or escalated to an analyst.
